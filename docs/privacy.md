# Tabfold privacy policy

_Last updated: October 10, 2026_

Tabfold is a tab manager. This policy covers the free version, which works entirely on your device.

## What Tabfold stores

When you save tabs, Tabfold stores, for each tab: its address, title, favicon link, pinned state, tab group name and colour, the keywords you pick, which window it was in, and when it was saved. It also stores your settings and recent snapshots of your open windows, used to recover your session after a crash or restart.

## Where it is stored

Only on your device, in your browser's own extension storage (IndexedDB and `chrome.storage`). Nothing is sent to Tabfold or anyone else. The free version makes no network requests at all.

## What Tabfold never does

- It never reads, collects or changes the content of web pages. It has no permission to access pages and runs no scripts in them.
- It never reads form data, passwords or your browsing history.
- It never sells or shares data.

## Keyword suggestions

Suggested keywords are worked out on your device from a tab's title and address only.

## Deleting your data

Delete sessions from the library (they stay in Trash for 30 days, then are removed), or use Settings › Data & privacy › Clear all data. Removing the extension deletes all of its data from your browser.

## Changes

If a future version adds optional features such as sync, this policy will be updated first, and those features will be opt-in.
