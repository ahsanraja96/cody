Tabfold

Fold your tabs, find them later.

Tabfold is a Chrome extension. One click folds your open tabs into a list and frees the memory they used. Tabs sort themselves by kind of site, or go in folders you name, and you can find any of them again by title, address or keyword.

Add to Chrome, free
  • Free holds 500 tabs and 3 folders, with no account.
  • Pro keeps every tab on every computer, backs them up daily and shares a folder as a link.
  • Pro AI sorts tabs into folders, and finds the one you mean when you ask.
  • Teams work from the same folders.
